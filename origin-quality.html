Our Story

More Than 75 Years of Coffee Heritage

Tasty Kaawa represents generations of family dedication to coffee farming in Bukomansimbi District, Uganda.

What began as a family tradition has evolved into a modern coffee enterprise focused on producing traceable Fine Robusta coffee for local consumers, specialty roasters and international buyers.

Every harvest reflects decades of accumulated knowledge, careful cultivation and respect for Uganda's coffee-growing heritage.

Why Uganda?

From Farm to Cup

Careful Harvesting & Processing

Every coffee cherry is handled with care to preserve quality, consistency and traceability.

Selective Harvesting

Only fully ripe cherries are hand-picked to ensure optimum sweetness, flavour development and consistent quality.

Processing

Depending on the desired cup profile, coffee is processed using Natural, Honey or Washed methods under carefully controlled conditions.

Drying & Storage

Coffee is dried to the required moisture level before being stored in clean, well-ventilated conditions to maintain freshness.

Processing Methods

Creating Distinct Cup Profiles

Natural

Whole cherries are carefully dried to produce coffees with fruity aromas, sweetness and a rich body.

Honey

Part of the fruit remains during drying, producing a balanced cup with pleasant sweetness and smooth acidity.

Washed

Fruit is removed before drying to achieve a bright, clean and consistent flavour profile.

Quality Assurance

Every lot is carefully monitored from harvest through drying, storage and preparation to ensure consistency for buyers.

  • Cherry selection
  • Moisture monitoring
  • Lot traceability
  • Careful storage
  • Buyer sample preparation
